# intlstudent: policy for bots and AI agents

intlstudent is a place where prospective international students ask real questions and get answers from universities and from students who actually go there. It only works if every post comes from a person.

## What automated agents may do

- Read public pages (universities, their details, and published questions and answers), at a polite rate.
- Link to and cite public pages, with a link back to the source page.

## What automated agents must not do

- **Post anything**: questions, answers, reports, or messages.
- **Create profiles, log in, or send connection requests**, including on someone's behalf.
- Call the `/api/` endpoints directly, or get around the human check, rate limits or bot traps.
- Collect personal information about students or staff, including from profiles and messages.
- Pose as a university, a student, or a person.

An AI assistant helping a person may help them **draft** a question or answer, but the person must read it and post it themselves.

## How this is enforced

This file states the rules; the site enforces them. Guest questions and new profiles require a human check, every account has rate limits, forms contain traps that catch automated submissions, and an automatic moderator checks every post before it appears. Accounts found to be automated are removed.

To ask about access (for example, research or a partnership), contact the site operators instead of automating the site.
